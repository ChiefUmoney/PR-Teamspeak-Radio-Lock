Project Reality Radio Lock 0.2.0 — no transmission duration cutoff

Close TeamSpeak 3. Open Project-Reality-Radio-Lock-0.2.0-win64.ts3_plugin and install/update it. Restart TeamSpeak and confirm Project Reality Radio Lock version 0.2.0 is enabled in Addons. Every participant should upgrade.

Keep [PR-RADIO] in protected channel topics. Keep native Push-To-Talk enabled and disable Add Voice Activity Detection. After joining, release PTT and wait about three seconds.

Hold PTT, wait for the permit beep, and talk for as long as you need. There is no maximum transmission length. Release PTT to free the channel.

If someone else presses PTT while the radio is occupied, only that person hears the custom busy sound and their outgoing audio is blocked. They must release and press again after the current speaker finishes. A held denied key does not auto-transmit later.

Connection-health checks remain, so a disconnect or lost controller can close the gate and require rekeying. The sound is also used for those existing denied/unavailable cases.

Automated tests passed: three simulated one-hour transmissions, 100 network-fault simulations, and a continuous 33-second compiled-DLL test crossing the former 30-second cutoff. Live two-user TeamSpeak voice has not been tested here. Follow README.md for a two-person check before general rollout.
